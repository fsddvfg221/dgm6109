Plot 2: Score vs. Happiness After

Hypothesis: As my golf score decreases, my happiness after the round tends to increase.
In addition: Play mode (color) and duration (circle size) are included to explore more detailed patterns.

Explanation of Why This Scatterplot Is Among My Best Work

This scatterplot presents three important variables that help analyze my golf performance and overall happiness: score (X-axis), happiness after the round (Y-axis), duration (bubble size), and whether I walked or used a cart (color). This chart directly examines my hypothesis that lower golf scores correlate with higher post-round happiness.

By encoding play mode through color and duration through bubble size, the visualization offers deeper insights into additional factors that may influence my performance or mood. For example, we can observe whether walking rounds (one color) tend to cluster around certain score–happiness combinations, or whether longer rounds (larger circles) show consistent patterns.

From a technical perspective, this scatterplot uses Array.sort() to manage the placement of circles so smaller bubbles are not hidden behind larger ones, improving clarity. It also uses Array.filter() to explore subsets of the data, such as isolating walking-only or higher-score rounds. These D3 techniques enhance readability and reveal more potential relationships, making this visualization one of my strongest analytical works.
