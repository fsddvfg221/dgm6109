Plot 1: Duration vs. Happiness After

Hypothesis: As the duration of my golf rounds increases, my happiness after playing increases.
In addition: Weather (color) and score (circle size) are included to explore additional correlations.

Why This Visualization Works

This visualization clearly presents multiple important data properties: duration (X-axis), happiness after playing (Y-axis), weather conditions (color), and score (circle size). It directly tests my hypothesis by examining whether longer rounds tend to result in higher post-round happiness.

Including weather as a color attribute and score as bubble size enhances the depth of the analysis. These additional properties help reveal potential secondary relationships. For instance, we can observe whether certain weather types cluster in high-duration, high-happiness regions or whether higher scores are associated with particular patterns in the chart.

From a technical standpoint, this visualization also applies Array.sort() to ensure that larger circles are drawn last, preventing them from hiding smaller circles behind them. Additionally, it uses Array.filter() to explore the distribution of longer rounds (over 250 minutes). These techniques improve readability and analytical clarity, making this scatterplot one of my strongest and most effective works.
