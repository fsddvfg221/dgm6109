How I Applied Array.sort() and Array.filter()

Array.sort() (Used in Plot 1)

In my first scatterplot, I used Array.sort() to sort the rounds by duration before drawing the circles. Sorting the data ensured that smaller bubbles were placed first and the larger ones were drawn last, preventing them from covering important data points. This made the relationship between duration and happiness much easier to see, especially when several values were close together on the chart.

Array.filter() (Used in Plot 2)

In my second scatterplot, I applied Array.filter() to highlight a specific subset of my data. I filtered rounds based on play mode (walking vs. cart) so I could examine the difference more clearly. Filtering reduced visual clutter and allowed me to compare score and happiness under different conditions in a more focused way.

Together, these techniques helped improve clarity and made both visualizations more effective for exploring the questions I was interested in.
