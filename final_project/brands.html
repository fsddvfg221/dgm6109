Famous Golf Brands

The golf equipment world is filled with iconic brands known for innovation, craftsmanship, and performance. Each brand brings something different—from classic forged irons to modern high-tech putters. This page highlights some of the most well-known golf brands, and takes a deeper look at LAB Golf, a company that has changed putter design with its unique lie-angle balanced technology.

Major Golf Equipment Brands

These companies are widely recognized in both amateur and professional golf. Each one is known for different strengths in clubs, accessories, or performance technologies.

Titleist

Known for tour-quality irons, premium wedges, and the #1 ball in golf, the Pro V1. Consistent performance and classic feel make Titleist a favorite among serious players.

TaylorMade

A leader in driver and wood technology. Known for distance, modern materials, carbon faces, and high-tech designs used by top tour players.

Callaway

Popular for forgiving drivers, irons, and Odyssey putters. Callaway blends performance with playability for golfers of all levels.

Ping

Famous for stability and consistency, especially in putters and irons. Ping equipment emphasizes forgiveness and reliability.

Mizuno

Known for forged irons with exceptional feel. Mizuno clubs are trusted by players who prefer a soft, precise feedback at impact.

Spotlight: LAB Golf

LAB Golf (Lie Angle Balance) is one of the most innovative putter brands in modern golf. Instead of following traditional putter design, LAB focuses on a unique engineering principle: creating putters that do not twist during the stroke. This allows golfers to swing the putter more naturally with fewer compensations.

What Is Lie Angle Balance?

LAB putters are engineered to stay square to the target line without manipulation. Traditional putters want to twist open or closed depending on weight distribution.

LAB’s lie-angle balanced design eliminates torque, helping players:

  • start putts on line more consistently
  • reduce face rotation during the stroke
  • focus on speed instead of manipulating the face

Popular LAB Models

LAB produces several high-performance models:

  • MEZZ.1 – Compact mallet with modern shape and extreme stability.
  • MEZZ.1 MAX – Larger version for maximum forgiveness.
  • DF 2.1 – The original oversized design known for ultra-straight roll.
  • B.2 – A full blade with lie-angle balance, very rare in putter design.

Why Players Choose LAB

LAB putters are ideal for golfers who struggle with consistency on the greens or want more automatic face control. Benefits include:

  • very stable through impact
  • reduces the need for timing in the stroke
  • excellent for straight-back/straight-through motions
  • custom lie angle fitting for precision setup

Many golfers report immediate improvements in starting line and distance control.

Who LAB Is Best For

LAB putters are great for:

  • golfers who miss putts because the face isn’t square at impact
  • players who want minimal rotation in their stroke
  • anyone who prefers stability over traditional feel
  • golfers open to unconventional designs

LAB isn’t a traditional brand—they focus purely on performance and engineering.

LAB Golf has developed a loyal following among amateur golfers and professionals looking for a more consistent putting motion. Although the shapes can look unusual at first, many players quickly grow confident in the performance and reliability of LAB’s designs.

A Brand for Every Type of Golfer

Whether you prefer the classic forged craftsmanship of Mizuno, the high-tech innovation of TaylorMade, the precision of Scotty Cameron, or the modern engineering of LAB Golf, every golfer can find a brand that matches their playing style and feel preferences.

Exploring different brands is part of the fun—and part of discovering the equipment that best supports your game.
