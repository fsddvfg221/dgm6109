About Square-Tee Golf

Square-Tee Golf is a personal project created by an amateur golfer who loves the game and loves experimenting with different golf gear.

Who Is Behind This Website?

My name is Rick, and I am an amateur golfer who spends a lot of time on the course and probably even more time thinking about golf equipment. Over the years, I have tried different drivers, iron sets, wedges, putters, bags, and accessories in the search for a setup that feels right for my swing and my game.

I am not a professional player or a club fitter. I am just someone who enjoys golf, learns by playing, and pays close attention to how different clubs and gear affect the experience on the course.

Why I Created Square-Tee Golf

When I started changing my own golf equipment more often, I realized two things:

  • Many golfers do not really understand what makes each club or accessory different.
  • It is easy to buy something that looks cool, but harder to know if it actually fits your game.

Square-Tee Golf was created to give golfers a clear, simple place to explore equipment in more detail. Instead of only looking at brand names or marketing, this site focuses on basic concepts, key specs, and how each type of club or accessory can match different playing styles.

What You Can Learn Here

The goal of this website is not to tell you what to buy, but to help you understand your options. On Square-Tee Golf, you can:

  • Learn the differences between drivers, fairway woods, hybrids, irons, wedges, and putters.
  • See how key specs like loft, shaft flex, head size, and forgiveness affect performance.
  • Explore common golf accessories such as bags, push carts, tees, gloves, and more.
  • Think about which combinations of clubs and gear might fit your own game and goals.

By understanding the basic ideas behind golf equipment, you can make more confident decisions and build a setup that actually supports the way you like to play.

Who This Site Is For

This site is especially for golfers who:

  • Are still experimenting with their clubs and set makeup.
  • Feel a bit overwhelmed by all the equipment choices and specs.
  • Want to understand their gear better instead of just following trends.

Whether you are new to the game or already play regularly, I hope Square-Tee Golf helps you slow down, compare options more clearly, and choose equipment that feels right for you.

A Personal Note

Every golfer’s journey is different. There is no single “perfect” set of clubs, but there is a setup that will feel more natural and more enjoyable for you. My hope is that this website makes it easier for golf lovers to understand their equipment in more detail and to choose a set that truly fits their game.

Thanks for visiting Square-Tee Golf, and I hope to see you out on the course.
