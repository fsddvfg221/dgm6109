Tees & Markers

Tees and ball markers are two of the simplest but most essential accessories in golf. They help golfers start each hole consistently and follow etiquette on the greens. This page explains how tees and markers are used, different types of each item, and where golfers in Vancouver can purchase them.

What Are Golf Tees?

A golf tee is a small stand that lifts the golf ball off the ground for your first shot on each hole. Tees help you achieve clean contact, especially with the driver, by positioning the ball at the correct height.

Tees are mainly used:

  • on the tee box at the start of each hole
  • for drivers, fairway woods, hybrids, or irons
  • to control height and launch angle depending on the club

Different Types of Tees

Tees come in different lengths and materials, each offering slightly different advantages.

Wood Tees

Traditional and affordable. Wood tees feel solid and natural, but they can break more easily when used with drivers.

Plastic Tees

More durable and often reusable for many rounds. They come in bright colors, making them easier to find on the tee box.

Brush or Specialty Tees

Designed to reduce friction at impact. Although not for everyone, some golfers like the consistent height and feel.

Common Tee Lengths

  • 4 inches: For drivers with large heads
  • 3.25 inches: Standard for most drivers
  • 2.75 inches: Fairway woods & hybrids
  • 2.1 inches: Irons or low tee shots

How to Use a Tee Properly

Tee height affects how the club meets the ball. General guidelines:

  • Driver: Half the ball should be above the top of the clubface.
  • Fairway wood: The ball should sit just above the ground.
  • Hybrid or iron: Tee it low—almost touching the grass.

Many amateur golfers tee the ball too low for driver, reducing distance. Experimenting with tee height can improve launch and consistency.

What Is a Ball Marker?

A ball marker is a small object used on the green to mark the position of your golf ball. This allows you to pick up and clean the ball or move it out of another golfer’s putting line.

Markers are commonly used to:

  • keep your ball's exact spot on the green
  • avoid blocking another player's line
  • clean dirt, grass, or water off the ball

Using a marker is part of golf etiquette and is required under the rules of golf.

Types of Ball Markers

Flat Metal Markers

Thin, round markers made from steel or aluminum. They lie flat on the green and are the most common type used by golfers.

Magnetic Hat Clip Markers

These attach to your hat brim and use a small magnetic coin. Convenient and easy to access.

Custom & Logo Markers

Many brands and golf courses offer custom designs. Some golfers collect them as souvenirs.

Plastic or Poker Chip Markers

Larger markers shaped like small poker chips. Fun designs, but can be too big if placed close to another player's putting line.

How to Use a Ball Marker Properly

Marking your ball on the green is simple:

  • Place the marker directly behind the golf ball.
  • Lift the ball straight up.
  • When replacing it, put the ball back in front of the marker exactly where it was.
  • If another golfer’s line is affected, politely move your marker one clubhead to the side.

This helps keep the green clear and ensures everyone has a fair putting line.

Tees and markers are inexpensive, so many golfers like to try different colors, sizes, and materials until they find what feels right.

Small Accessories, Big Impact

Tees and markers may be small, but they are essential parts of a golfer’s setup. Choosing the right tee height and using markers properly can make your round smoother and help you play with better etiquette on the greens.

As with any part of golf, experimenting with different styles can help you discover what fits your game the best.
