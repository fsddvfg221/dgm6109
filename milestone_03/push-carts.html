Push Carts

Push carts help golfers walk the course more comfortably by carrying the weight of the golf bag. They reduce strain on your shoulders, improve endurance, and make walking a more enjoyable part of the game. This page introduces manual vs electric push carts, major brands available in Vancouver, and takes a closer look at the popular Alphard Club Booster V2.

Why Use a Push Cart?

Walking 18 holes can be physically demanding, especially on hilly courses. A push cart allows golfers to walk without carrying the weight of their bag, reducing fatigue and improving posture.

Push carts also keep your bag steady and organized, protect your clubs, and give easier access to:

  • balls, tees, and gloves
  • rangefinders, scorecards, umbrellas
  • water bottles and snacks

For golfers who play often, using a push cart can help prevent back and shoulder strain.

Manual vs. Electric Push Carts

Manual Push Carts

These carts require you to push or pull them, but modern designs roll smoothly and are easy to maneuver. They are lightweight, fold compactly, and are usually the most affordable option.

Electric / Motorized Carts

Electric carts use a battery-powered motor to move on their own, letting you walk freely without pushing. They are ideal for hilly courses or golfers who want maximum comfort. Many have remote controls or automatic follow modes.

Manual carts are simple and reliable, while electric carts offer luxury and convenience—especially on long or difficult courses.

Popular Push Cart Brands

Some of the most common push cart brands you will see in Vancouver include:

  • Clicgear – Very durable, compact folding carts with strong brakes.
  • BagBoy – Smooth wheels and lots of storage features.
  • Sun Mountain – Lightweight carts with stable designs.
  • Axglo – Canadian company known for easy folding mechanisms.
  • Motocaddy – Premium electric carts with GPS integration (higher budget).
  • Alphard – Electric conversion kits (Club Booster V2) that convert manual carts into motorized carts.

Spotlight: Alphard Club Booster V2

The Alphard Club Booster V2 is one of the most popular electric push cart solutions on the market. Instead of buying a full electric cart, the V2 transforms your existing manual push cart into a fully motorized one—making it extremely cost-effective.

How It Works

The Club Booster V2 replaces the two back wheels of your existing push cart with a motorized axle. The battery powers the unit, providing smooth forward movement on all terrain, including hills.

Main Features

  • Remote-controlled steering
  • Auto downhill control
  • Easy battery removal and charging
  • Compatible with many major cart brands
  • Strong hill-climbing ability

Who It’s For

The V2 is perfect for golfers who:

  • already own a manual push cart
  • want electric convenience at lower cost
  • walk hilly courses (like Northlands or Fraserview)
  • prefer remote control over pushing

Why It’s Popular in Vancouver

Vancouver courses often have steep slopes and long walking distances. The V2 helps players conserve energy on hills while still enjoying the benefits of walking. Its weather-resistant build also suits Vancouver’s rainy climate.

Many golfers consider the Alphard V2 to be the best “value electric cart” option because it adds motorization without requiring a brand-new cart purchase.

Finding the Right Push Cart

Whether you prefer a simple manual cart or a high-tech electric solution like the Alphard V2, having a push cart that matches your walking habits can make every round more enjoyable. For many golfers in Vancouver, walking the course is part of the experience—and the right push cart makes that walk easier, smoother, and more fun.
